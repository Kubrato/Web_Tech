About This Project

Istanbul on Film:
Foreign Cinema in the City

This is a web-based cinematic tourism project. It was built as the final project for the Master's Degree in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge at the University of Bologna. The project looks at Istanbul as a film location — a city that has been used in spy films, action films, historical dramas, and mystery films for more than sixty years.

01

Project Overview & Author

This project shows Istanbul not just as a background for films, but as a real partner. Its streets, buildings, and history shape some of the most famous scenes in foreign films from 1963 to 2016.

The site is not a film database or a normal tourist guide. The content is organised around two narratives: two different ways to experience the same city. The first one, Pursuit & Passage, is an action-style journey through twelve locations. The second one, Istanbul Through Time in Cinema, compares how the same places were filmed in four different periods (1963, 2012, 2014, 2016). It has sixteen locations.

The site supports two main use cases: planning at home with the map and the narrative reader, and visiting the locations on foot with the same content as a guide.

This is a 1-person individual project. The two-theme design (Mid-Century Cinema / Heritage) and the two-narrative structure (Pursuit & Passage / Through Time) are deliberate scope choices for a solo project: two coherent authorial voices rather than one diluted compromise.

02

Selected Films

Five films were chosen for three reasons: they all use real Istanbul locations, they cover different time periods between 1963 and 2016, and they belong to different genres. Together they show the many ways foreign cinema has used the city.

03

The Two Narratives

◈ Pursuit & Passage

An action-style journey in four chapters: Surveillance → The Chase → The Search → Confrontation. The user visits twelve locations in order. Each one has a short text and a small editorial note. The journey shows two kinds of pursuit at the same time: the fast chases of Bond and Bryan Mills, and the quiet search of Joshua Connor for his lost sons.

Films: From Russia with Love · Taken 2 · Skyfall · The Water Diviner · Inferno

◇ Istanbul Through Time

A comparison journey through sixteen locations by period: 1960s → 2012 → 2014 → 2016. The same domes, bridges, and bazaars come back again and again. The buildings do not change, but the camera does. The journey shows how Istanbul's place in cinema has changed: from Cold-War mood to fast action, from post-war memory to mystery puzzles.

Films: From Russia with Love · Skyfall · Taken 2 · The Water Diviner · Inferno

04

Academic Context

The project was developed for the course Information Modeling and Web Technologies, part of the Master's Degree in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge at the University of Bologna.

The project tries to answer a few questions that are common in digital humanities: How can a narrative help organise a cultural dataset? How can a website be useful both for visitors and for researchers? And how can we make a website that is informative, critical, and visually pleasant at the same time?

The topic — cinematic tourism — was chosen because more and more people travel to places they saw in films. Cinema shapes how we see a city and creates a kind of cultural memory tied to specific locations.

05

Suggested One-Day Itinerary

All sixteen locations sit inside the historic peninsula and the Üsküdar shoreline — close enough to walk between most of them in a single day. The schedule below is one tested order, starting at Sultanahmet at opening time and ending across the Bosphorus at sunset. Times include short visits and average walking transfers; allow extra slack for queues at Hagia Sophia and the Basilica Cistern.

On the Map page, the same sequence is drawn as a dashed line connecting markers in order: 1 → 12 when the Pursuit & Passage filter is active, and 1 → 16 for Through Time. The walking plan below is a third, geographic ordering — the most efficient way to see every location on a single day.

  • 09:00 — Hagia Sophia · Inferno · arrive at opening to skip the queue (≈ 30 min inside).
  • 09:45 — Basilica Cistern · Inferno · directly across Sultanahmet Square (≈ 30 min underground).
  • 10:30 — Sultan Ahmet Mosque · The Water Diviner · two minutes south of the Cistern.
  • 11:00 — Firuz Aga Mosque · From Russia with Love · small Ottoman mosque on Divan Yolu, a brief photo stop.
  • 11:30 — Gülhane Park · The Water Diviner · descend through the park toward the seawall.
  • 12:00 — Sirkeci Railway Station · From Russia with Love · the Orient Express terminus; lunch nearby.
  • 13:00 — Spice Bazaar · Taken 2 · spices and lokum under the L-shaped market hall.
  • 13:45 — Eminönü Square · Taken 2 · ferry crowds, simit carts, and the Yeni Mosque steps.
  • 14:15 — Galata Bridge · Taken 2 · cross on foot, fishermen on the upper deck.
  • 14:30 — Grand Bazaar · Skyfall · uphill to Beyazıt; enter via the Nuruosmaniye gate.
  • 15:30 — Grand Bazaar Rooftop · Skyfall · view of the leaded domes from outside the Beyazıt exit.
  • 16:00 — Istanbul University · Inferno · the Beyazıt main gate, two minutes away.
  • 16:45 — Legacy Ottoman Hotel · Taken 2 · walk down to Sirkeci; classical façade.
  • 17:15 — Deutsche Orientbank Hotel · Skyfall · the Hobyar quarter, just north of Sirkeci.
  • 18:00 — Maiden's Tower · From Russia with Love · ferry from Üsküdar; arrive for sunset light.
  • 19:30 — Haydarpaşa Train Station · The Water Diviner · final stop on the Asian shore; the fitting end.
