Istanbul on Film: Foreign Cinema
in the City

Five international productions. Six decades of cinema. One city filmed by spies, fugitives, fathers and scholars. This exhibition walks you through the real Istanbul locations behind From Russia with Love, Taken 2, Skyfall, The Water Diviner and Inferno — on screen and on foot.

Pursuit & Passage

Action-Driven Cinematic Journey

Walk in the footsteps of spies, fugitives and seekers. From the bazaar chase to a quiet mosque courtyard, from a Bosphorus tour to an underground cistern — twelve locations, four chapters, one journey through the city as a stage.

Istanbul Through Time in Cinema

Analytical Comparative Journey

Compare how Istanbul has been filmed across six decades. The same domes and bridges return again and again, but the camera changes — from the slow atmosphere of 1963, to the kinetic chases of 2012, the post-war memory of 2014, and the puzzle-solving of 2016.
