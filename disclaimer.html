01

Purpose of the Site

The purpose of this web site is to explore various types of typographic and layout style for specialized tourist visits, as an end-of-course project for the “Information Modeling and Web technologies” course of the Master’s Degree in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge of the University of Bologna, under the supervision of prof. Fabio Vitali.

02

Original Sources of the Documents

The documents contained in this web site have been selected for their length and complexity from the public catalogue records, encyclopaedic entries and production notes of the five films featured in the exhibition. Their publication here is not intended to be an alternative to or a replacement for their original locations:

  • From Russia with Love (1963) · dir. Terence Young · Eon Productions / United Artists · IMDb ↗ · Wikipedia ↗
  • Taken 2 (2012) · dir. Olivier Megaton · 20th Century Fox · IMDb ↗ · Wikipedia ↗

04

Accuracy & Access

Coordinates and access information are provided in good faith for educational purposes; visitors should verify opening hours and access conditions directly with each site. The narrative texts are interpretive readings of the films and their locations, not authoritative production records. Some locations may have restricted public access; this exhibition does not encourage trespass or unauthorised entry.
